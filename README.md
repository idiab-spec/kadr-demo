# Kadr (كادر)

A live camera guide for car photos. It shows people where to stand, tells them one thing to fix at a time
(in English or Arabic), and takes each photo by itself once the shot is right.

This is a pitch demo. It runs entirely in the browser: car detection uses MediaPipe's object detector on the
device, and the light, sharpness and movement checks are plain image maths. No photo leaves the phone.

Open it on a phone over https, allow the camera, and point it at a car. Without a car, use
"No car nearby? Use a video" and pick a video of someone walking around a car.
